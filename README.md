# ☕ Coffee Consumer Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on strategic consumer segmentation and behavioral analysis within the coffee industry.

The analysis compares:
- Value-driven mass-market consumers
- Premium lifestyle-oriented consumers

using index-based segmentation and consumer behavior analytics.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze consumer demographics & preferences
- Identify behavioral differences
- Understand media & shopping patterns
- Develop strategic recommendations for premium market expansion

---

## 🧠 Methodology
The project used:
- Index-based analysis
- Consumer segmentation
- Behavioral profiling
- Comparative analysis
- SMART strategic framework

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Mass-market consumers prioritize affordability & practicality
- Premium consumers value experience & lifestyle
- Digital engagement strongly impacts premium brand perception
- Significant opportunity exists in aspirational urban segments

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
- Digital transformation
- Premium product positioning
- Experience-based marketing
- Urban affluent targeting

---
